Every pose is two files drawn from one shape: the outline, and the mask the fill is clipped to. The highlighted pair is on screen now.

Swapping in the real frames

  1. Put each pose in loader/frames/ as name.outline.svg and name.mask.svg (PNG with a clear background works too).
  2. List the names in loader/frames/manifest.js: the dance order, the waiting poses, and the finale pose.
  3. Keep every frame on the same canvas size with the feet on the same line, so the fill level matches across poses.
